V1.5: Uploading a valid CSV immediately ranks eligible companies and automatically runs Agentic Contact Intelligence for Tier 1–2 prospects. Contact Intelligence is a post-ranking layer and does not change Readiness, Confidence, Rank or Tier. It prioritizes verified current Training leadership, then specialized Training leadership, broader L&D/capability leadership, and practitioners as fallback. If a current person cannot be verified, the interface shows the ideal role instead of inventing a contact.

Company research rules: Stakeholder fields mean a relevant, targetable EON stakeholder/function is evidenced — not merely that the company has employees in that function. For triggers, No means research supports absence of a qualifying current event; if research is inconclusive, use Unknown. Unknown is neutral for readiness but lowers confidence.

Readiness = 35% Company Fit + 40% Stakeholder Fit + 25% Trigger Readiness. Confidence is displayed separately and never changes Readiness or Tier. Tiers are assigned relative to the eligible companies in the uploaded file.
